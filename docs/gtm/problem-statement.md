# Problem Statement

Compliance teams spend 200–400 hours per audit cycle manually collecting evidence, mapping policies to controls, and chasing down artifacts across fragmented tools. This burden grows with every new framework and every quarterly review.

## The four costs of manual compliance

**1. Time drain**
A single SOC 2 Type II audit requires evidence for 60+ controls across security, availability, and confidentiality. Teams copy screenshots from AWS Config, pull Jira tickets, export GitHub logs, and paste everything into spreadsheets.

**2. Continuous drift**
Controls that pass in Q1 drift by Q3. Access lists change, new services launch, and policies become outdated. Manual audits are point-in-time snapshots, not continuous assurance.

**3. Vendor risk accumulation**
Every third-party SaaS tool used for evidence storage or collaboration adds a vendor to your compliance boundary. Each one requires its own SOC 2 review, data processing agreement, and security questionnaire.

**4. Auditor friction**
Auditors request evidence in specific formats. Teams scramble to reconstruct audit trails from multiple systems, often missing items or producing inconsistent documentation. Remediation suggestions are buried in email threads.

## What teams actually need

- Automated mapping from policy documents to control frameworks
- Live evidence collection from the tools they already use
- Continuous gap scoring instead of quarterly panic
- Audit-ready output in their own storage, under their own retention policies

AuditFlow AI is built to eliminate the manual loop without sending your data to a third party.
